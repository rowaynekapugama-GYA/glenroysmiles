FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What's included in the New Patient Package?

New patients receive a comprehensive dental check-up, scale and clean, digital x-rays and fluoride treatment — everything needed to assess your oral health and create a personalised treatment plan.

How does the no gap offer work?

If you have an Australian health fund with dental extras cover, we'll process your claim on the spot with HICAPS and waive the remaining gap — so your first visit costs you nothing out of pocket. The offer runs until 31 August for new patients. If you don't have health insurance, the same package is just $185.

Can I claim through my health fund?

Yes. We accept all health funds and process claims on the spot with HICAPS — just bring your health fund card to your appointment.

Is my child eligible for bulk-billed dental care?

Eligible children may receive up to $1,132 in selected dental services every 2 calendar years under the Medicare Child Dental Benefits Schedule. Call us on (03) 9008 4561 and we'll check your child's eligibility. Conditions apply.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — we offer DentiCare payment plans, Afterpay and Humm, so you can spread the cost of treatment in a way that suits your budget.

Where are you located and is there parking?

We're at Unit 2/830 Pascoe Vale Rd, Glenroy VIC 3046 — just 2 minutes' walk from Glenroy train station, with ample parking nearby.

Can my whole family be seen on the same day?

Yes. We offer family appointments where each family member can be seen consecutively — just mention your preference when booking.
